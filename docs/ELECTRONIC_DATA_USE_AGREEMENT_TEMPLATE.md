## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to permit limited data sharing and use for clinical research, public-benefit analytics, and quality-improvement initiatives supported by Cingulum Foundation Inc.'s research data platform.

## 3. Data Scope

The Hospital may disclose the following data classes to Cingulum Foundation Inc.:

- Demographic and enrollment data
- Intake and questionnaire responses
- Study metadata and outcomes data
- Procedure/trial consent metadata
- Photos and videos uploaded through approved workflows

Data elements are limited to those required for approved research protocol(s) and institutional approvals.

## 4. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

Cingulum Foundation Inc. may use disclosed data solely to:

1. Operate and support approved research projects;
2. Perform protocol-defined analytics and reporting;
3. Generate aggregate or de-identified findings for program oversight;
4. Support multi-site project coordination and monitoring.

Cingulum Foundation Inc. shall not use data for unrelated commercial advertising, unauthorized profiling, or resale.

## 5. Privacy, Security, and HIPAA Alignment

Both parties agree to implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards including:

- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Role-based access controls
- Audit logging for access and modification
- Minimum necessary access policy
- Incident response and breach notification procedures

Where Protected Health Information (PHI) is involved, use/disclosure must comply with applicable HIPAA requirements and any governing Business Associate Agreement(s).

## 6. De-identification and Limited Data Set Handling

If data is shared as a Limited Data Set or de-identified dataset, Cingulum Foundation Inc. agrees:

- not to attempt re-identification except where explicitly authorized by law and protocol;
- not to contact individuals except through approved, consented workflows;
- to apply suppression/aggregation controls in reporting outputs where required.

## 7. Patient Rights and Consents

Hospital remains responsible for:

- obtaining required patient authorizations and informed consent;
- confirming eligibility for data sharing under applicable law and policy;
- honoring withdrawal and restriction requests according to protocol and legal requirements.

## 8. Subcontractors and Service Providers

Cingulum Foundation Inc. may engage subprocessors only under written agreements imposing equivalent data protection and confidentiality obligations.

## 9. Data Retention and Destruction

Upon expiration or termination, Cingulum Foundation Inc. will return or securely destroy shared data as required by law, protocol, contractual terms, and record-retention obligations.

## 10. Audits and Compliance Cooperation

The parties will reasonably cooperate with compliance and regulatory inquiries related to this Agreement, including provision of audit evidence where legally permissible.

## 11. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with written notice as defined below:

- Notice period: `[NUMBER]` days
- Immediate termination for material breach, unlawful use, or security risk

Termination does not eliminate obligations for confidentiality, security, and legally required retention.

## 12. Limitation, Liability, and Governing Law

To be completed with counsel:

- Limitation of liability terms
- Indemnification terms
- Governing law and venue

## 13. Electronic Signatures and Records

The parties agree to execute this Agreement electronically.  
Electronic signatures, timestamps, signer identity records, and associated audit logs are deemed valid and enforceable to the extent permitted by applicable law.
